\begin{appendixitem}{Field norm}
    For a field extension \( K \hookrightarrow L \), for each \( \alpha \in L \), we can consider
    the \( K \)-linear endomorphism of multiplication by \( \alpha \):
    \[
        \left[\times \alpha\right] : L \rightarrow L.
    \]
    If the extension is \( K \)-finite-dimensional (then it is algebraic), we can compute its
    characteristic polynomial \( P_{\operatorname{char}, \left[\times \alpha\right]}\left(X\right)
    \in K\left[X\right] \). In particular, we can compute its determinant (the constant term of
    \( P_{\operatorname{char}, \left[\times \alpha\right]}\left(X\right) \)), which defines the
    \textit{field norm}:
    \[
        N_{L/K} : L \rightarrow K, \quad N_{L/K}\left(\alpha\right) := {\det}_{K}\left(\left[\times
        \alpha\right]\right).
    \]
    Since \( \forall \beta \in L,\ \left[\times \alpha \beta\right] = \left[\times \alpha\right]
    \circ \left[\times \beta\right] \), we have
    \[
        N_{L/K}\left(\alpha \beta\right) = N_{L/K}\left(\alpha\right) N_{L/K}\left(\beta\right).
    \]
    In the case where \( L/K \) is Galois (which is equivalent to \( K \hookrightarrow L \) being a
    normal and separable field extension), we must have that the roots in \( L \) of the minimal
    polynomial of \( \alpha \) in \( K\left[X\right] \) satisfy
    \[
        \left\{ \sigma\left(\alpha\right) \mid \sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}\left(L/K\right)
        \right\} = \operatorname{Root}_{P_{\min, \alpha}\left(X\right)}\left(L\right) =
        \operatorname{Root}_{P_{\min, \alpha}\left(X\right)}\left( K^{\mathrm{alg}} \right),
    \]
    where the first equality follows from standard Galois theory, and the second from the fact that
    the extension is normal. Now, because \( P_{\min, \alpha}\left(X\right) =
    P_{\min, \left[\times \alpha\right]}\left(X\right) \) (a mental exercise), we have:
    \[
        \operatorname{Root}_{P_{\operatorname{char}, \left[\times \alpha\right]}\left(X\right)}\left(
        K^{\mathrm{alg}} \right) =
        \operatorname{Root}_{P_{\min, \left[\times \alpha\right]}\left(X\right)}\left(
        K^{\mathrm{alg}} \right) = \operatorname{Root}_{P_{\min, \alpha}\left(X\right)}\left(
        K^{\mathrm{alg}} \right),
    \]
    where the first equality follows since the roots (in \( K^{\mathrm{alg}} \)) of the minimal
    polynomial of an endomorphism (on a finite-dimensional vector space) are precisely its
    eigenvalues. Combining:
    \begin{equation}
        \label{A.2.1} \tag{1}
        \operatorname{Root}_{P_{\operatorname{char}, \left[\times \alpha\right]}\left(X\right)}\left(
        K^{\mathrm{alg}} \right) = \left\{ \sigma\left(\alpha\right) \mid \sigma \in
        \operatorname{Gal}\left(L/K\right) \right\}.
    \end{equation}
    If \( \alpha \) generates the extension, i.e. \( L = K\left(\alpha\right) \), or equivalently
    \( \deg\left( P_{\min, \alpha}\left(X\right) \right) = \left[L:K\right] \), then, since the
    extension is finite and Galois, we have \( \left| \operatorname{Gal}\left(L/K\right) \right| =
    \left[L:K\right] \). From this, as \( P_{\min, \alpha}\left(X\right) \) is separable, it follows
    that for each \( \sigma, \sigma' \in \operatorname{Gal}\left(L/K\right) \), \( \sigma \neq
    \sigma' \) implies \( \sigma\left(\alpha\right) \neq \sigma'\left(\alpha\right) \). Thus:
    \begin{equation}
        \label{A.2.2} \tag{2} \left|\left\{ \sigma\left(\alpha\right) \mid \sigma \in
        \operatorname{Gal}\left(L/K\right)
        \right\}\right|=\left[L:K\right]=\deg\left(P_{\operatorname{char}, \left[\times \alpha\right]}\left(X\right)\right).
    \end{equation}
    From \eqref{A.2.1} and \eqref{A.2.2}, we obtain:
    \[
        P_{\operatorname{char}, \left[\times \alpha\right]}\left(X\right) =
        \prod_{\sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}\left(L/K\right)} \left( X - \sigma\left(\alpha\right)
        \right)\footnote{In fact, this holds even if \( \alpha \) does not generate the extension
        (but still \( L/K \) is finite and Galois), as one can show that the multiplicities of each
        root of the characteristic polynomial \( \theta \in
        \operatorname{Root}_{P_{\operatorname{char}, [\times \alpha]}(X)}\left( K^{\mathrm{alg}}
        \right) \) coincide with the number of automorphisms of \( L \) fixing \( K \) and sending
        \( \alpha \) to \( \theta \), that is, \( \left| \left\{ \sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}(L/K)
        \mid \sigma(\alpha) = \theta \right\} \right| \).},
    \]
    and so, by Vieta's formula:
    \begin{equation}
        \label{appendix:field-norm:A.2.3} \tag{**} N_{L/K}\left(\alpha\right) =
        \prod_{\sigma \in \operatorname{Gal}\left(L/K\right)} \sigma\left(\alpha\right).
    \end{equation}
\end{appendixitem}
