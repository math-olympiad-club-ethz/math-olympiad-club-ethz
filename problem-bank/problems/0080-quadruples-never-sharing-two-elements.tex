\begin{problem}
Fix an integer $m$ and look at a set $S$ of $5^m$ elements. We look at a family $\mathcal{F}$ of subsets of $S$ of size 4, such that for any two subsets in the family, their intersection does not have size 2. What is the maximum value of $|\mathcal{F}|$, i.e. what is the maximum number of subsets with four elements we can choose, such that the property holds?
\end{problem}
