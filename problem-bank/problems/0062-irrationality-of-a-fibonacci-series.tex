\begin{problem}
Let $F_n$ be the $n$th Fibonacci number defined by $F_1 = 1$, $F_2 = 1$ and for all $n > 2$, $F_n = F_{n-1} + F_{n-2}$. Prove that 
\[
\sum_{n\geq 1}\left( \frac{1}{9}\right)^{F_{n+2}}
\]
is an irrational number but not a transcendental number.
\end{problem}
