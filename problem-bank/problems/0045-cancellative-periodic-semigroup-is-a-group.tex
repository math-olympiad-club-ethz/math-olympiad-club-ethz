\begin{problem}
    Let \( \left(S,\cdot\right) \) be a non-empty magma; that is \( S \) be a non-empty set, and:
    \begin{align*}
        \cdot:S\times S &\longrightarrow S\\
        (a,b) &\mapsto \cdot((a,b))\overset{not}{=}a\cdot b
    \end{align*}
    be a function (an internal binary operation).
    \\\\
    Suppose it satisfies the following:\\
    \\
    -\( \left(S,\cdot\right) \) forms a semi-group i.e \( \cdot \) is associative;
    \[
        \forall a,b,c\in
        S,\cdot(\left(a,\cdot\left(\left(b,c\right)\right)\right)=\cdot\left(\left(\cdot\left(a,b\right),c\right)\right)\ i.e.\ a\cdot\left(b\cdot
        c\right)=\left(a\cdot b\right)\cdot c
    \]
    -\( \cdot \) is injective in left and right coordinate \( \forall a,b,c\in S \);\\
    \[
        \cdot\left(\left(a,b\right)\right)=\cdot\left(\left(a,c\right)\right)\Rightarrow
        b=c\ i.e.\ a\cdot b=a\cdot c\Rightarrow b=c
    \]
    \[
        \cdot\left(\left(b,a\right)\right)=\cdot\left(\left(c,a\right)\right)\Rightarrow
        b=c\ i.e.\ b\cdot a=c\cdot a\Rightarrow b=c
    \]
    \\
    -\( \forall a\in S, a^{\mathbb{N}_{\geq 1}}:=\left\{a^n |\ n\in\mathbb{N}_{\geq 1}\right\} \) is
    finite, where,
    \[
        a^1:=a\ and\ for\ n>1,\ a^n=a\cdot a^{n-1}
    \]
    Note that this definition is independent where we start to compute since \( \cdot \) is
    associative.\\\\
    Show \( \left(S,\cdot\right) \) is a group.
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
    In order to show \( \left(S,\cdot\right) \) is a group we need to start by showing that
    \( \left(S,\cdot\right) \) is a monoid; that is a semi-group where there exist an identity
    element for \( \cdot \) . We know \( \left(S,\cdot\right) \) is a semi-group. We search an
    identity element.\\\\
    Let \( a\in S \) which exists since it is non-empty, now we know \( a^{\mathbb{N}_{\geq 1}} \)
    is not infinite which means the following statement is true:
    \[
        \neg\left(\forall m',n'\in\mathbb{N}_{\geq 1}, m'< n'\rightarrow a^{m'}\neq a^{n'}\right)
    \]
    Which means \( \exists m,n\in\mathbb{N}_{\geq 1}, m< n\wedge a^m=a^n \). We define:
    \[
        e_S:=a^{n-m}\in S
    \]
    We see that since \( \cdot \) is an associative binary operation that:
    \[
        a=a^1=a^{m-\left(m-1\right)}=a^{n-\left(m-1\right)}=a\cdot a^{n-m}=a\cdot e_S
    \]
    Therefore again for the same reason we have:
    \[
        a\cdot e_S^2=a\cdot \left(e_S\cdot e_S\right)=\left(a\cdot e_S\right)\cdot e_S=a\cdot e_S
    \]
    Using injectivity in left coordinate:
    \[
        e_S^2=e_S
    \]
    This identity will be the key in our context to show \( e_S \) is the identity element for
    \( \left(S,\cdot\right) \) i.e. \( \forall b\in S, e_S\cdot b=b=b\cdot e_S \).\\\\
    Let \( b\in S \), one has using that \( S \) is a semi group:
    \[
        b\cdot e_S=b\cdot e_S^2=\left(b\cdot e_S\right)\cdot e_S
    \]
    and
    \[
        e_S\cdot b=e_S^2\cdot b=e_S\cdot\left(e_S\cdot b\right)
    \]
    using injectivity in the right and left coordinate respectively: \( b=b\cdot e_S \) and
    \( b=e_S\cdot b \) as desired.\\
    We have therefore shown \( \left(S,\cdot,e_S\right) \) is a monoid, to show it is a group we
    need to show \( \forall g\in S,\exists h\in S,g\cdot h=e_S=h\cdot g \) (each element have right
    and left inverse which are the same). Let \( g\in S \):\\
    if \( g=e_S \) we have \( e_S^2=e_S \) so \( e_S \) has an inverse which is itself. \\
    If \( g\neq e_S \) then \( \exists r,s\in\mathbb{N}_{\geq 1}, r< s\wedge g^r=g^s \):
    \[
        e_S\cdot g=g=g^1=g^{r-\left(r-1\right)}=g^{s-\left(r-1\right)}=g^{s-r}\cdot g
    \]
    using injectivity in right coordinate: \( g^{s-r}=e_S \) we know \( s-r\geq 1 \) if \( s-r=1 \)
    then \( g=e_S \) a contradiction therefore \( s-r\geq 2 \) so that \( s-r-1\geq 1 \) and we
    have:
    \[
        g\cdot g^{s-r-1}=e_S=g^{s-r-1}\cdot g
    \]
    so that \( g \) has an inverse which is \( g^{s-r-1} \). Hence \( \left(S,\cdot,e_S\right) \) is
    a group.
\end{solution}
