\begin{problem}
Let $n\geq 2$ be an integer. Choose uniformly $X_1, \ldots, X_n$ from $[0, 1]$. Let $p_n$ be the probability that $X_i + X_{i+1} \leq 1$ for all $i = 1, \ldots, n-1$. Prove that $\lim_{n \to \infty} p_n^{1/n}$ exists and compute it.
\end{problem}
