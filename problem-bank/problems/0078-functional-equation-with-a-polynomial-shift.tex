\begin{problem}
Let \(\mathbb{R}^+\) be the set of all positive real numbers. 
Find all functions \(f : \mathbb{R}^+ \to \mathbb{R}^+\) and polynomials \(P(x)\) with non-negative real coefficients such that \(P(0)=0\) and which satisfy
\[
    f\bigl(f(x) + P(y)\bigr) = f(x - y) + 2y
\]
for all real numbers \(x > y > 0\).
\end{problem}
