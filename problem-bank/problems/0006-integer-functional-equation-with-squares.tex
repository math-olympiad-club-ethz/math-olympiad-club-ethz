\begin{problem}
    Find all functions \( f : \mathbb Z \to \mathbb Z \) such that
    \[
        \left(f\left(x + y\right)\right)^2 = f\left(x^2\right) + f\left(y^2\right)
    \]
    for all \( x, y \in \mathbb Z \).
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
    Plug in \( y=-x \), and let \( c=f\left(0\right) \). We have \( f\left(x^2\right) =
    \frac{c^2}{2} \). Plugging in \( y=0 \), we have \( f\left(x\right)^2 = \frac{c^2}{2} + c \).
    Plugging in \( x=0 \) in this gives \( c^2=\frac{c^2}{2}+c \), hence \( c=0 \) or \( c=2 \). Now
    if \( c=0 \), \( f \equiv 0 \) is the only solution. Else we have \( f\left(x\right) = \pm 2 \)
    for all \( x \). This means \( f\left(x^2\right) + f\left(y^2\right) = 4 \), always, so for all
    \( x \) being perfect squares, \( f\left(x\right)=2 \). So the solution in this case becomes
    \( f\left(x\right) = 2 \) for all perfect square \( x \) and \( \pm 2 \) for all other \( x \).
    It is easy to check that the solutions mentioned above work. Thus:
    \[
        \left\{ f : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z} \,\middle|\, \forall x,y \in \mathbb{Z}
        \ \left(f\left(x + y\right)\right)^2 = f\left(x^2\right) + f\left(y^2\right) \right\}
    \]
    \[
        = \left\{ \underline{0} \right\} \cup \left\{ 2\mathbbm{1}_{A} -
        2\mathbbm{1}_{\mathbb{Z} \setminus A} \,\middle|\, A \subset \mathbb{Z} \text{ with }
        \square_{\mathbb{N}} \subset A \right\}
    \]
    where \( \square_{\mathbb{N}} := \left\{ n^2 \mid n \in \mathbb{N} \right\} \).
\end{solution}
