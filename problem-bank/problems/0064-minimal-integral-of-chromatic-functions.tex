\begin{problem}
    A function \( f : \left[0, 1\right] \rightarrow \mathbb{R} \) is \textit{chromatic} if:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item for all \( x, y \in \left[0, 1\right], |f\left(x\right) - f\left(y\right)| \leq |x -
            y| \), and
        \item \( \int_{0}^{1} f\left(x\right) dx = 1/2 \).
    \end{itemize}


    Over all pairs \( f, g : \left[0, 1\right] \rightarrow \mathbb{R} \) of chromatic functions,
    what is the minimum value of

    \[
        \int_{0}^{1} f\left(x\right)g\left(x\right) dx?
    \]
\end{problem}
\begin{solution}
    Notice that each chromatic function is continuous and therefore its Riemann integral is well
    defined. Define
    \[
        \mathcal{A} := \left\{ h \mid h : \left[0, 1\right] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}
        \text{ is chromatic} \right\} \subset \mathcal{F}\left(\left[0,1\right], \mathbb{R}\right).
    \]
    Observe that for each \( h \in \mathcal{A} \) we have \( 1 - h \in \mathcal{A} \), so that
    \[
        1 - \_ : \mathcal{A} \rightarrow \mathcal{A}
    \]
    is well defined and clearly \( \left(1 - \_\right) \circ \left(1 - \_\right) =
    \mathrm{Id}_{\mathcal{A}} \), hence it is a bijection. Furthermore, if \( f, h \in
    \mathcal{A} \), then
    \[
        \int_{0}^{1} f\left(x\right) \left( 1 - h\left(x\right) \right) \, dx = \frac{1}{2} -
        \int_{0}^{1} f\left(x\right) h\left(x\right) \, dx,
    \]
    so that in total we have
    \[
        \inf_{f, h \in \mathcal{A}} \int_{0}^{1} f\left(x\right) h\left(x\right) \, dx =
        \inf_{f, h \in \mathcal{A}} \int_{0}^{1} f\left(x\right) \left( 1 - h\left(x\right) \right)
        \, dx = \frac{1}{2} - \sup_{f, h \in \mathcal{A}} \int_{0}^{1} f\left(x\right)
        h\left(x\right) \, dx.
    \]
    If we can find \( f, h\in\mathcal{A} \) such that \( \int_{0}^{1} f\left(x\right)
    h\left(x\right) \, dx \) is maximal, then we know that \( \displaystyle \int_{0}^{1}
    f\left(x\right) \left( 1 - h\left(x\right) \right) \, dx \) is minimal.

    \textcolor{red}{to finish}
\end{solution}
