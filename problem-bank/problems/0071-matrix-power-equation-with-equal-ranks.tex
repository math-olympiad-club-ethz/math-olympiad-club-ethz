\begin{problem}
 Let \( n \geq 1 \) be an integer and \( A, B \in \text{Mat}_{n \times n}(\mathbb{R}) \) be two \( n \times n \)-matrices with \(\text{rank}(A) = \text{rank}(B)\). Assume there exists an integer \( k \geq 1 \) such that  
\[A^{k+1}B^k = A.\]  
Show that then also  
\[B^{k+1}A^k = B.\]
\end{problem}
