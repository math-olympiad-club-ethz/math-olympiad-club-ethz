Welcome to the Math Olympiad Club at ETHZ!

We are a group of passionate mathematicians dedicated to solving challenging problems and exploring the beauty of mathematics. Our club focuses on preparing for and participating in mathematical competitions.

Our mission is to encourage problem-solving skills, and provide a space for students to meet and challenge themselves with Olympiad-style problems. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced problem-solver, we hope you'll find something in our meetings to inspire you.

Our problem bank collects the problems of our sessions: search it by topic, make your own problem set as a PDF, or propose a problem.
